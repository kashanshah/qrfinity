# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Teknoffice Technologies Inc. runs QRfinity. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never collect. The short version: we collect as little as we can, we never store IP addresses, and we don't sell or rent data to anyone.

## Making static codes

The generator runs in your browser. What you type, and any logo you add, never leaves your device unless you sign in and save it. We don't log your content.

Designs you save without an account stay in your browser's storage.

## Your account

When you create an account we store:

- your **email address**, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google;
- a **session** record so you stay signed in: a random token, when it expires, and your browser's user-agent string. We don't store your IP address.

We use your email only to sign you in and to tell you about your codes, for example if we turn one off. No newsletters or marketing unless you ask for them.

## Your codes

For each dynamic code we store its name, short link, destination, design, folder and tags, and a history of destination changes. If you add a logo to a saved design, we store the logo image.

## Scans of dynamic codes

When someone scans a dynamic code, we record:

- the time;
- the approximate location (country, region and city) that our network provider, Cloudflare, works out from the connection;
- the kind of device, operating system and browser, worked out from the browser's user-agent;
- the website that referred the visit, if any (the site's name only, not the full address);
- a **visitor hash**, used only to count unique scans. It's made from the visitor's IP address, user-agent and the code, mixed with a random value that changes every day and is deleted after 36 hours. Once that value is gone, the hash can't be traced back to anyone or linked across days.

**We never store IP addresses**, in scan data, sign-in records or logs. Scan data is kept for 90 days.

We set no cookies on the short domain (qrfinity.link), and people who scan your codes see no tracking or ads from us.

## Reports

If you report a QR code, we store what you send. Your email is optional; if you give it, we use it only to follow up, and we never share it with the code's owner.

## Cookies

When you're signed in we set one cookie to keep your session. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run third-party analytics on qrfinity.com.

## Service providers

We use these companies to run QRfinity. They process data only on our instructions:

- **Vercel**: hosts qrfinity.com.
- **Cloudflare**: runs the short links, scan analytics, and the bot check (Turnstile) on sign-in and report forms.
- **Neon**: our database.
- **Resend**: sends sign-in and notification emails.
- **Google**: if you choose Google sign-in; and Google Web Risk, which checks code destinations for phishing and malware (it receives the destination address, nothing about you).

Some of these providers process data outside Canada, including in the United States. {/* TO CONFIRM regions. */}

## How long we keep data

- Account data: until you delete your account.
- Scan data: 90 days.
- Abuse reports and our audit log of moderation actions: as long as needed to prevent abuse. {/* TO CONFIRM a fixed period. */}

When you delete your account we remove your email and sign-in records and delete your codes. We keep a record that each short link existed, without your details, so it's never given to someone else.

## Your rights

You can see and download all your codes and their history at any time (Account → Export your data), correct them in your dashboard, and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by writing to privacy@qrfinity.com. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and, for people in the EU and UK, the GDPR.

## Children

QRfinity isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and email account holders about important changes before they take effect.
