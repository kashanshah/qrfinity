QR codes let the shop window, the shelf, the bag and the receipt keep working after the customer walks away: reviews and how-to videos on the shelf, loyalty sign-ups at the till, your social pages on the bag, returns information on the receipt. Make them dynamic to change them with the season and see which ones customers use. QRfinity’s codes are free and never expire.

## A window that works after closing time

A code in the window can open your online shop, this week’s offers or a booking page while the door is locked. With a dynamic code, change it for every season and campaign without a new window sticker, and see how many people scanned overnight.

## Count every placement

Give the window, the till, the bags and each shelf their own dynamic code. They can all go to the same page; each still counts its own scans, so you can see which placements work. We don’t store anyone’s IP address.

## Where to put codes

- **Shelf labels and tags:** 2 to 2.5 cm.
- **Counter and till:** 4 cm on a stand.
- **Windows:** 10 to 15 cm, at eye level, inside the glass.
- **Market stalls:** 5 cm on the price board.
