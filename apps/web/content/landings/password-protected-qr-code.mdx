A password-protected QR code asks for a password before it opens anything. On QRfinity you can add one to any dynamic code, whether it opens a link or a contact card, and change or remove it at any time without reprinting. It’s free on one code, and plans from $5 a month cover more.

## How does a password-protected QR code work?

The printed code holds a short link, such as `qrfinity.link/` followed by a few characters. When someone scans it:

1. Their phone opens a simple page on our short domain asking for the password.
2. They type it and tap the button.
3. If it’s right, they go straight to your destination, or see your contact card. If not, they’re asked again.

The password is asked on every scan. Nothing is stored on the phone, so there are no cookies, no app and no account for the scanner.

## What can I use it for?

- **Staff and member pages.** Rotas, training videos, price lists or a members’ area that shouldn’t be public, behind a code on the staff room wall.
- **Event extras.** Slides, recordings or photo galleries for people who attended, printed on the badge or the last slide.
- **Private contact details.** A contact card with your mobile number for clients, not for anyone who photographs your sign.
- **Early access.** A sale or launch page shared with a list before it opens to everyone.

<Callout>
  A password keeps casual scanners out. It isn’t the right tool for medical records, banking or
  anything that needs proper accounts and audit trails. Protect those on the destination site
  itself.
</Callout>

## How do I make one?

1. Create a dynamic code with the generator on this page and save it to your free account.
2. Open the code in your dashboard and find **Password protection**.
3. Type a password of 4 to 64 characters and save. Scans ask for it within about a minute.

You can change the password whenever you like, for example after a staff member leaves, and the printed code keeps working.

## What do I see in analytics?

Scans are counted when someone opens the password page, so you see every scan, including people who didn’t know the password. Devices, countries and cities work as they do for any dynamic code, with up to 2 years of history. We don’t store IP addresses, and we never store the passwords people type.
