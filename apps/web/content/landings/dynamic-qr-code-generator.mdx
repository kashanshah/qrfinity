## What is a dynamic QR code?

A dynamic QR code holds a short link instead of your destination. Ours look like `qrfinity.link/` plus a few characters. When someone scans the code, their phone opens the short link, our redirect service looks up where the code should go, and forwards them there, usually in a fraction of a second.

Because the destination lives in your account instead of in the printed pattern, you can change it whenever you like. A menu link, a PDF, a campaign page, a booking form: point the same printed code at something new without reprinting anything. And because every scan passes through the short link, we can count it.

If you’re not sure you need one, the [static vs dynamic guide](/guides/static-vs-dynamic-qr-codes) explains when each makes sense.

Because we decided they wouldn’t, and wrote it down. We never deactivate, pause or meter a free code, and that promise is in our [terms](/terms).

A QRfinity code stops redirecting in only two cases:

- **You pause it.** Scanners see a page saying the code is paused by its owner, until you turn it back on.
- **It breaks our [acceptable use policy](/acceptable-use).** We screen destinations with URL-reputation checks and disable codes used for phishing, malware or similar harm, to protect the people who scan them.

## How do you make a dynamic QR code?

1. **Choose Dynamic.** It’s already selected in the generator above.
2. **Enter your link.** Paste the address you want people to reach. You can change it later.
3. **Design it.** Pick colours, add a frame with a call to action like “Scan for the menu”, add your logo if you like. Watch the scan check as you go.
4. **Save your dynamic code.** Choose “Save your dynamic code” and sign in with a link sent to your email, or with Google. The account is free and takes under a minute.
5. **Download and print.** Use SVG, PDF or EPS for print, PNG for screens.

From then on, your dashboard shows every code, its short link, its scans and its current destination.

## Which types of QR code can be dynamic?

| Type | What the scan opens | Why dynamic helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Link | Any web page, PDF, form or file | Swap the page without reprinting |
| WhatsApp | A chat with your number, message ready | Change the number or message later |
| Location | Your address in the phone’s maps app | Update it if you move |
| App store | Your app’s App Store or Google Play page | Point it at a new listing if your app changes |
| Social | Your profile on Instagram, YouTube and others | Change the handle or platform later |

Wi-Fi logins, contact cards, plain text, email, SMS and calendar events are always static. Phones read those straight from the code, which also means they work offline and never depend on us.

## What do we do with scan data?

We count scans without collecting personal data:

- **No raw IP addresses are stored.** Unique scans use a short fingerprint made with a salt that changes every day and is then deleted.
- **No cookies on the redirect**, and no ads.
- **Bots and link previewers are excluded**, so pasting your short link into a chat doesn’t inflate your numbers.

The [scan tracking guide](/guides/how-to-track-qr-code-scans) explains exactly what is and isn’t measured.
