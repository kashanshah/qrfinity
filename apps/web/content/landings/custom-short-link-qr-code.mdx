A custom short link replaces the random characters in a dynamic QR code’s link with words you choose, like `qrfinity.link/joes-menu`. Print it under the code and people can read it, remember it and type it when they can’t scan. On QRfinity it’s free on one dynamic code, and the code never expires.

## Why use a custom short link?

Every dynamic code holds a short link. Normally it ends in a few random characters, which is fine for a phone camera and useless for a person. A readable link helps when:

- **Someone can’t scan.** A cracked camera, a glare-covered window or a code printed too small. A link they can type is the backup.
- **People hear it rather than see it.** On the radio, in a podcast or from the stage, “qrfinity.link/joes-menu” is easy to say.
- **Trust matters.** People are rightly wary of scanning unknown codes. A link that says what it is, printed under the code, shows where they’re going before they scan.
- **You want it to look finished.** On a business card or shop window, a branded word reads better than a random string.

## How do I get one?

1. In the generator on this page, choose a dynamic code and enter your destination.
2. Save it to your free account. On the last step, type your short link in **Custom short link**.
3. Create the code. The link is checked to make sure it’s free and safe, then it’s yours.

It can’t be changed after you create the code, because printed copies depend on it. Where it sends people can change at any time.

<Callout>

## Good short links

- **Short and specific:** `joes-menu`, `spring-sale`, `book-a-table`.
- **No dates you’ll regret:** `menu` will outlive `menu-2026`, since the destination can change.
- **Easy to say:** avoid hyphens in odd places and look-alike characters such as `l1` or `0o`.
