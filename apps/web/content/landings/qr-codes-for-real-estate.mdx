A QR code on a yard sign, flyer or business card lets buyers open the listing, the photos or your contact details on the spot. Make the sign code dynamic and it outlives the listing: when the home sells, point it at the next one. QRfinity’s dynamic codes are free, unlimited and never expire.

## Where do QR codes work best in real estate?

| Where | What the code opens | Static or dynamic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yard and window signs | The listing page or a photo tour | Dynamic, so the sign can be reused |
| Flyers and feature sheets | The listing, or a booking page for viewings | Dynamic, so you can count scans |
| [Business cards](/qr-code-generator/business-card) | Your [contact card](/qr-code-generator/vcard) with a Save contact button | Dynamic, so you can update it |
| Open house handouts | The listing plus a [map](/qr-code-generator/location) to the next open house | Either |
| Mailers and postcards | Your homes for sale, or a home valuation page | Dynamic, one per campaign |

## Why make the sign code dynamic?

A static code holds the listing’s address directly and can never change. That’s fine for a one-off flyer, but signs and riders get reused. A dynamic code holds a short link you control:

- **Reuse the sign.** Point it at the next listing when this one sells.
- **Fix mistakes after printing.** Wrong link on 500 flyers? Change the destination instead of reprinting.
- **See what works.** Each code counts its own scans, so you can tell which sign, mailer or street brings buyers.

## How do I make one?

1. Choose **Dynamic** in the generator on this page and paste the listing’s link.
2. Add your brokerage colours or logo. The scan check warns you before you download anything that might not scan.
3. Save it to your free account and download it. Use one code per sign or campaign if you want to compare them.

For your own details, make a dynamic contact card instead: it opens a clean page with your name, phone, email and website, and a Save contact button that adds you straight to the buyer’s contacts.

## What size and format for print?

- **Yard signs:** 25 to 30 cm wide for reading from the sidewalk. Dark code on a light panel, with a clear margin.
- **Flyers and cards:** at least 2 cm wide; 2.5 to 3 cm on a business card is comfortable.
- **Files:** a high-resolution PNG works for most printing. Sign shops often ask for vectors, and SVG, PDF and EPS files come with a paid plan.

The [print size guide](/guides/qr-code-size-for-print) explains the rule behind these numbers.
