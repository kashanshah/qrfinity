## What a Calendly QR code does

A Calendly QR code opens your booking page in one scan. The person sees your free times, picks one and gets a calendar invite, with no back-and-forth emails. It turns a business card, flyer or conference badge into a booked meeting.

Consultants and coaches put them on cards and slides, salespeople on badges and brochures, tutors and therapists on flyers, and recruiters on job ads.

## How to make a Calendly QR code

1. **Copy your booking link.** In Calendly, copy the link of the event type you want people to book.
2. **Paste it into Website address.** The Calendly logo is added automatically.
3. **Choose static or dynamic.** Dynamic lets you swap the meeting type later and counts scans.
4. **Make it yours.** Pick colours and a frame like “Book a 15-minute call”.
5. **Test it, then download or save.** Scan the code and check the right meeting type opens.

## Design and print tips

- **Say how long and how much.** “Free 15-minute call” tells people exactly what they’re booking.
- **On business cards, 2 to 2.5 cm is enough,** with a clear margin.
- **On slides, leave it up during questions,** at least a metre wide on the screen.
- **Check your availability first.** A code that opens a page with no free times wastes the scan.
