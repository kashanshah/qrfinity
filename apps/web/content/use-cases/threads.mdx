## What a Threads QR code does

A Threads QR code opens your Threads profile in one scan, so people can follow you there without searching. It suits anyone building a following on Threads alongside Instagram: shops, creators, writers and local businesses.

## How to make a Threads QR code

1. **Check that Social and Threads are selected.** This page loads the Social type with Threads chosen as the Platform.
2. **Enter your username.** With or without the @; it’s the same as your Instagram username.
3. **Choose static or dynamic.** Static works forever with nothing in between. Dynamic lets you change where it goes later and counts scans.
4. **Make it yours.** The Threads logo is already in the middle. Pick colours and a frame like “Follow us on Threads”.
5. **Download or save.** Download a static code right away, or save a dynamic code to your free account.

## Design and print tips

- **Say why to follow.** “Daily specials and behind the scenes” beats the logo alone.
- **Print the @username too.**
- **Black and white suits Threads,** and scans best.
- **Size it for the distance.** 2.5 cm on cards and receipts, 4 to 5 cm on counters, 20 cm or more on posters.
