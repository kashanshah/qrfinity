## What a Zoom QR code does

A Zoom QR code joins your meeting from a phone in one scan: no meeting ID to type, no passcode to copy. It’s the quickest way to get people from a printed agenda, a poster or a slide into a call.

Teachers and lecturers use them for online office hours, community groups and churches for hybrid meetings, event organisers for virtual rooms, and conferences to let people in another room follow a session.

## How to make a Zoom QR code

1. **Copy the join link.** In Zoom, copy the invite link of the meeting or webinar, ideally the one with the passcode included.
2. **Paste it into Website address.** The Zoom logo is added automatically.
3. **Keep it dynamic.** This page starts with a dynamic code, so you can paste in next week’s link without reprinting.
4. **Make it yours.** Pick colours and a frame like “Join online”.
5. **Save and test.** Save the code, scan it with your phone, and check it opens the right meeting.

## Design and print tips

- **Print the time and time zone** next to the code, so people know when to scan.
- **Use a recurring meeting** for weekly sessions: one link, one code, no updates needed.
- **After the meeting, point it somewhere useful,** such as the recording or next month’s session.
- **On slides, leave it up** for latecomers, at least a metre wide on the screen.
