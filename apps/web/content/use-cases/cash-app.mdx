## What a Cash App QR code does

A Cash App QR code opens your $Cashtag in one scan, with the amount filled in if you set one. The payer confirms and pays from their Cash App balance or card. No typing your $Cashtag, no sending money to someone with a similar name by mistake.

Barbers and nail techs put them by the chair, bands on merch tables, and street vendors on their carts.

## How to make a Cash App QR code

1. **Check that Payment and Cash App are selected.** This page loads the Payment type with Cash App chosen.
2. **Enter your $Cashtag.** With or without the $, or paste your cash.app link.
3. **Add an amount, or leave it open.** Set one for a fixed price; leave it empty for tips.
4. **Make it yours.** The Cash App logo is already in the middle. Pick colours and add a frame such as “Tips welcome”.
5. **Scan it, then download or save.** Check that your $Cashtag opens on your phone, then download the code or save it as a dynamic code.

## Design and print tips

- **One code per price.** For a menu of services, a code for each price is quicker than asking people to type amounts.
- **Print your $Cashtag too.** It lets payers check the name Cash App shows before they confirm.
- **Protect it from stickers.** Laminate the sign or print the code into it, and check it now and then for anything stuck on top.
- **Size it for the distance.** 3 cm suits a counter card, 5 cm a flyer, and 20 cm or more a poster.
