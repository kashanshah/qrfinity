## What a link-in-bio QR code does

A link-in-bio QR code opens the page that lists all your links: your shop, latest video, newsletter, socials and booking page. One scan takes people to everything, and you update the list whenever you like without touching the code.

Musicians put them on merch and stage banners, creators on stickers and business cards, small shops on packaging, and speakers on their last slide.

## How to make a link-in-bio QR code

1. **Copy your page’s address.** For example linktr.ee/yourname.
2. **Paste it into Website address.** Linktree links get the Linktree logo automatically.
3. **Choose static or dynamic.** Dynamic lets you switch to a different service later and counts scans.
4. **Make it yours.** Pick your brand colours and a frame like “All my links”.
5. **Download or save.** Download a static code right away, or save a dynamic code to your free account.

## Design and print tips

- **Put your most important link first** on the page: most people tap the top one.
- **Say what’s there.** “Music, merch and tour dates” beats “Links”.
- **On merch, test the fabric.** Print a sample and scan it; stretchy or textured fabric can distort small codes.
- **Size it for the distance.** 2.5 cm on cards and stickers, 20 cm or more on a stage banner.

## Static or dynamic?

<Recommendation pick="either">
A static code holds your page’s address and works for as long as the page does. Choose dynamic if you might move to another service or your own website, and to count scans from each place you print it.
