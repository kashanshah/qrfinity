## What an X QR code does

An X QR code opens your profile in one scan, so people can follow you without typing a handle full of underscores or picking you out of look-alike accounts.

Speakers put them on their last slide, journalists and writers on business cards, brands on packaging and event screens, and support teams on receipts for quick help.

## How to make an X QR code

1. **Check that Social and X (Twitter) are selected.** This page loads the Social type with X chosen as the Platform.
2. **Enter your handle.** Type it with or without the @, or paste your profile link.
3. **Choose static or dynamic.** Static works forever with nothing in between. Dynamic lets you change where it goes later and counts scans.
4. **Make it yours.** The X logo is already in the middle. Pick colours and a frame like “Follow for updates”.
5. **Download or save.** Download a static code right away, or save a dynamic code to your free account.

## Design and print tips

- **Say what people get.** “Live updates from the conference” beats a bare logo.
- **Print the @handle as well,** for anyone who would rather search.
- **On slides, leave it up.** Make the code at least a metre wide on screen and keep it there during questions.
- **Black and white suits X.** Its own colours are black and white, which also scan best.
