## What a QR code on a business card does

A business card QR code saves your details into someone’s phone with one scan, so your card is no longer the only record of you. Most cards end up in a drawer or a bin within a week. A saved contact stays, with your name spelled right and your number in the right field.

You can also point the code at a website, a portfolio or an online profile, which suits designers, photographers, consultants and anyone whose work is easier to show than describe.

## How to make a business card QR code

1. **Start with the contact details.** This page opens the Contact type. Fill in First name, Last name, Company and Job title, then Mobile, Email and Website.
2. **Or switch to Link.** If you’d rather send people to an online profile, choose the Link tile under “1. What should the code open?” and paste the address.
3. **Set the print width.** Enter the size the code will be on your card, such as 2.5 cm. The scan check warns if the squares will be too small to print reliably.
4. **Match your card, then download.** Use your card’s ink colour for the code, add a small frame such as “Scan to save”, then download an SVG, PDF or EPS for the printer, or a print-size PNG.

## Planning the card

A standard card is 3.5 × 2 in in North America and 85 × 55 mm in much of the world. A 2.5 cm code takes up a little over a quarter of the card’s height on the front, which is why many people use the back. Allow for the quiet zone too: a clear border of at least 4 modules around the code, which also keeps it away from the trim line where the cutter can wander by a millimetre or two.

## Design and print tips

- **Keep the contact data lean.** Each extra field makes the pattern denser. A name, one phone number, an email and a website are usually enough.
- **Print dark on light.** Light-coloured codes on dark card stock, and codes on metallic or foil finishes, often fail. If your card is dark, print the code on a light panel.
- **Avoid spot UV or embossing on the code.** Raised or glossy coatings distort the pattern and reflect light. Keep finishes away from the code area.
- **Say what it does.** “Scan to save my contact” under the code takes up one line and tells people it’s not just another website.
- **Order a proof.** Scan the printed proof on two different phones before approving the full run.
