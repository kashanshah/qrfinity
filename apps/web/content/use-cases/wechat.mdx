## What this page does

WeChat and Alipay make their own QR codes: your contact card, a group invitation, an Official Account, or a code to collect payments. Only the apps can make them, and they all look the same: black and white, with no hint of whose code it is.

This page restyles them. You add a screenshot of your code, your browser reads it, and you get a new code that holds exactly the same thing, in your colours, with the WeChat or Alipay logo in the middle and a frame with your own words. People scan it with WeChat or Alipay as usual, and it opens the same contact, group or payment page.

## How to restyle your code

1. **Get your code from the app.** Open your QR code in WeChat or Alipay and take a screenshot.
2. **Add the screenshot.** Choose the image, drop it on the box above, or paste it. You can also point your camera at a code on another screen.
3. **Check what was found.** The page tells you what kind of code it is and shows exactly what it holds, which stays the same.
4. **Make it yours.** The WeChat or Alipay logo is already in the middle. Pick colours and a frame, or switch the logo to your own.
5. **Scan it, then download.** Scan the new code with your phone first. Then download a PNG for screens, or SVG, PDF or EPS for print with a plan.

## Which WeChat codes work

- **Your contact code** opens your profile so people can add you. It keeps working unless you reset your code in WeChat.
- **Official Account codes** open the account so people can follow it. Some are made to be temporary and expire after up to 30 days; if a restyled one stops working, the original has too.
- **Group codes** open a group invitation, but only for 7 days after WeChat made the code. Use them for something short, like this week’s event, and make a new one after.
- **WeChat Pay and Alipay codes** for collecting payments work the same way after restyling. Check the name the app shows before you print.

## Design and print tips

- **Keep it dark on light.** Coloured codes scan fine, but the modules need to be clearly darker than the background. The scan check flags anything under 3:1 contrast.
- **Say what the code is for.** “Add me on WeChat”, “Join our group” or “Pay here” gets more scans than a bare code.
- **Write the frame in Chinese if your readers do.** “扫码加我微信” or “扫一扫，付款” works as frame text. It’s drawn as shapes, so it looks the same in every download and prints without any fonts.
- **Size it for the distance.** 2.5 cm suits a business card, 4 to 5 cm a flyer or table card, and 20 cm or more a poster read from 2 metres away.
