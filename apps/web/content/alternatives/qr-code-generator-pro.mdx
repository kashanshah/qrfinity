## What happens when the trial ends?

The trial lasts 14 days, needs no credit card and gives you every feature. {/* source: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/pricing/, seen 2026-10-16 */} You don’t have to cancel it, and nothing is charged if you let it lapse. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665044670477-Do-I-need-to-cancel-my-free-trial-if-I-don-t-want-to-continue-using-QR-Code-Generator-PRO, seen 2026-10-16 */}

What changes is your dynamic codes. Their support article says that when the trial ends, dynamic codes are deactivated and anyone who scans one lands on a service page. Static codes keep working. Your account data is kept, but you can’t see it until you upgrade. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665046137613-What-happens-to-my-account-and-QR-Codes-when-the-trial-expires, seen 2026-10-16 */} Another help page puts it simply: a paid subscription is needed to generate and track dynamic codes, and codes are paused if the plan is no longer active. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665233505293-How-many-QR-Codes-do-I-get-in-my-plan, seen 2026-10-16 */}

To their credit, the way back is clear. Upgrading the same account reactivates the codes “regardless of how long your subscription has been expired”. A new account, or a new code with the same content, does not. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665046137613-What-happens-to-my-account-and-QR-Codes-when-the-trial-expires, seen 2026-10-16 */}

Cancelling a paid plan works in a similar way. Codes keep redirecting until the end of the paid period plus 30 days, during which you can’t edit them. After that the account is locked and dynamic codes go to a service page. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665399659277-How-do-I-cancel-my-QRCG-by-Bitly-subscription, seen 2026-10-16 */} An older guide on their site says codes stay active for three years after cancellation, so their pages differ; the support article is the more specific of the two. {/* source: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/guides/qr-code-editing/, seen 2026-10-16 */}

With QRfinity there is no trial to end. Dynamic codes are free and unlimited with an account, and we never pause or meter them for not paying.

## How to switch

1. **Leave your static codes alone.** Static codes from QR Code Generator PRO keep working after any trial or plan ends, so there’s no need to replace them. {/* source: https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665040427405-Will-my-Static-QR-Codes-expire-at-the-end-of-the-free-trial, seen 2026-10-16 */}
2. **Plan for your dynamic codes.** They can’t move to another provider, because the short link inside each code belongs to QR Code Generator PRO. Keep your plan active until you’ve replaced the printed codes, or swap them for QRfinity codes over time as you reprint.
3. **Use the 30-day window if you cancel.** Codes keep redirecting for 30 days after the paid period ends, which gives you a little time to finish replacing them.
4. **Make new codes with QRfinity.** Create a free account, make a dynamic code with the same destination, test it with your phone, and print it on your next run.
