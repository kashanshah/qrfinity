## What happens when the trial ends?

QRFY’s trial lasts 7 days and covers both dynamic and static code types. It collects no card at sign-up, so, in QRFY’s words, “it is physically impossible for us to charge you”. {/* source: https://qrfy.com/en, seen 2026-10-16 */} You don’t need to cancel anything if you decide not to continue. {/* source: https://support.qrfy.com/en/articles/6998445-do-i-need-to-cancel-my-free-trial-if-i-don-t-want-to-continue-using-qr-code-generator-pro, seen 2026-10-16 */}

The catch is what happens to anything you printed in those 7 days. Their help centre says dynamic codes made during the trial “will stop working (deactivate) once the trial ends” and send scanners to a service page instead. QRFY emails you 48 hours before the trial ends and again after the codes are deactivated. {/* source: https://support.qrfy.com/en/articles/6998440-what-happens-to-my-account-and-qr-codes-when-the-trial-expires, seen 2026-10-16 */}

To bring the codes back you upgrade the same account; a new account or a new code won’t revive them. {/* source: https://support.qrfy.com/en/articles/6998446-how-can-i-reactivate-my-qr-codes, seen 2026-10-16 */} How long you have to do that is less clear. The pricing page says your data and designs are stored for 30 days, while a support article on reactivation says there is no time limit. {/* source: https://qrfy.com/pricing, seen 2026-10-16 */} If you’re relying on either, confirm with QRFY.

Buying a plan is a commitment of at least 3 months. Support confirms there is no monthly option: contracts run for a quarter, half a year or a year. {/* source: https://support.qrfy.com/en/articles/6998461-do-you-offer-individual-plans-and-payment, seen 2026-10-16 */} If you later cancel, codes stay active until the paid period ends, and unused months aren’t refunded. {/* source: https://support.qrfy.com/en/articles/6998475-what-happens-to-my-account-and-qr-codes-if-i-cancel-my-subscription-renewal, seen 2026-10-16 */}

QRfinity skips all of this. There’s no trial clock and no contract, and our dynamic codes keep working whether or not you ever pay.

## How to switch

Start with an inventory of what you’ve printed.

**Static QRFY codes** don’t need replacing. QRFY says they “will remain active forever”, including after a trial ends. {/* source: https://qrfy.com/pricing, seen 2026-10-16 */}

**Dynamic QRFY codes** can’t be moved. Each one points to a short link that only QRFY controls, so another service can’t take it over. If you’re on a paid plan, use the rest of your term to replace printed codes with QRfinity ones. If your trial has already ended, those codes are no longer redirecting, so replace the ones that matter first.

**For anything new**, make a dynamic code with QRfinity, scan it with your own phone, and print. You can change where it points later without reprinting.
