## Can you edit or track QRCode Monkey codes?

No, and that follows from what the codes are. QRCode Monkey makes static codes: the destination is written into the pattern itself. Its own site says the codes don’t stop working after a certain time, but the content can’t be changed once they’re created. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-monkey.com/, seen 2026-10-16 */} Because a static code goes straight to its destination, there’s also no point in between where a scan could be counted, so QRCode Monkey has no scan analytics. {/* source: https://pdfgeneratorapi.com/blog/top-10-free-qr-code-generators-in-2026, seen 2026-10-16 */}

For a lot of uses that’s fine. A Wi-Fi card, a vCard or a link to a page that will never move doesn’t need to be dynamic.

It matters when something changes after printing: a menu URL, a campaign landing page, a shop that moves domain. With a static code you reprint. A third-party review says that turning on QRCode Monkey’s “statistics and editability” option sends you to QR Code Generator PRO, a separate paid product with a 14-day trial. {/* source: https://pdfgeneratorapi.com/blog/top-10-free-qr-code-generators-in-2026, seen 2026-10-16 */} That product’s cheapest plan with dynamic codes, Starter, includes 2 of them for about USD 9.99 a month billed annually, at the time of checking. {/* source: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/pricing/ and https://www.qr-code-generator.com/blog/qr-code-generator-vs-qr-tiger/, seen 2026-10-16 */}

QRfinity puts both kinds in one place. Static codes need no account. Dynamic codes need a free account, have no trial, and keep working whether or not you ever pay. You can change the destination at any time and see total and unique scans, devices and countries for the last 90 days.

## How to switch

Your QRCode Monkey codes are static, so there’s nothing to migrate or keep paying for. They’ll keep working as long as the destination they point to exists.

Replace a code only when you have a reason to: the destination is changing, or you want to know how many people scan it. In that case, make a QRfinity dynamic code that points to the same page, check it with your phone, and use it on your next print run. After that, you can change where it goes without reprinting.

For new static codes, either tool works. For anything printed in quantity or likely to change, a dynamic code saves you a reprint later.
