## What happens when the trial ends?

QR TIGER doesn’t run a time-limited trial for its free plan. It describes it as “a subscription plan that’s totally free with no expiry”, with no credit card, unlimited static codes and 3 dynamic codes limited to 500 scans each. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/software-subscription, seen 2026-10-16 */} That is more generous than most of the market, and worth saying plainly.

Two things are less clear. One QR TIGER page says free dynamic codes “might expire, depending on your subscription plan”, which doesn’t sit easily with the no-expiry wording above. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/generate-a-free-qr-code, seen 2026-10-16 */} And we couldn’t find a page that says what a free code does once it reaches 500 scans. If you plan to print a free QR TIGER dynamic code on something busy, such as packaging or a shop window, ask them first.

Paid plans are spelled out in the FAQ: “Dynamic QRs have unlimited scans with a valid subscription. If your plan expires, it will stop working.” It adds that data is retained “for a maximum period of 1 year”, after which it may be removed. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/faq, seen 2026-10-16 */} Their terms say the same, and note the period can be extended on request. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/terms-and-conditions, seen 2026-10-16 */}

QRfinity has no plan for a code to expire with. Dynamic codes are free with an account, unlimited in number and scans, and we never pause them for not paying.

## How to switch

- **Static codes:** keep them. QR TIGER says its static codes never expire and “will remain functional”, so there’s nothing to replace. {/* source: https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/do-free-qr-codes-expire, seen 2026-10-16 */}
- **Free-plan dynamic codes:** if a code is close to its 500-scan limit, replace it first. Make a QRfinity dynamic code pointing to the same page and swap it in wherever the old one is printed.
- **Paid dynamic codes:** they can’t be transferred, because the short link inside each one belongs to QR TIGER. Keep your plan running until the printed codes are replaced, or replace them gradually as materials are reprinted.
- **New codes:** make them with QRfinity, so you never have a renewal deciding whether your print still works.
