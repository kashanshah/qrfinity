PDF417 is the stacked barcode on boarding passes, ID cards and shipping documents. It holds hundreds of characters of text in rows of bars, and ordinary laser scanners and phone scanner apps read it. Type or paste your text and download a scan-tested PDF417. PNG and JPG are free.

## When to use PDF417

- **Tickets and boarding passes:** a booking reference, name and seat in one barcode.
- **Shipping and customs documents:** sender, recipient and contents for scanning at a depot.
- **Inventory and asset records:** a full description that a scanner can read without a database.
- **Event and visitor badges:** name, company and access level.

## Tips for PDF417

- **Plain text works best.** Letters, digits and punctuation pack tightly; long runs of digits pack tighter still.
- **Print it wide enough.** Keep the narrowest bar at least 0.25 mm. A long text makes a taller code, not a wider one.
- **Keep the margins.** Leave blank space on both sides of the barcode.
- **Scale evenly.** Resize the whole image without changing its proportions.

For a smaller square code that holds the same text, use [Data Matrix](/barcode-generator/data-matrix) or [Aztec](/barcode-generator/aztec).
