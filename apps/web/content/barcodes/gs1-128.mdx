GS1-128 is the barcode on pallet labels, shipping cases and healthcare packaging: Code 128 with GS1 application identifiers, so one barcode can carry the product’s GTIN, a batch number, an expiry date and a weight. Type each field with its identifier in brackets, and we check it, add the separators and test the scan. PNG and JPG are free.

## Common application identifiers

| AI     | Means                             | Format              |
| ------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| (00)   | SSCC, the pallet or parcel serial | 18 digits           |
| (01)   | GTIN of the trade item            | 14 digits           |
| (02)   | GTIN of the items inside a case   | 14 digits           |
| (10)   | Batch or lot number               | Up to 20 characters |
| (11)   | Production date                   | YYMMDD              |
| (15)   | Best before date                  | YYMMDD              |
| (17)   | Expiry date                       | YYMMDD              |
| (21)   | Serial number                     | Up to 20 characters |
| (30)   | Count of items                    | Up to 8 digits      |
| (37)   | Count of trade items in a case    | Up to 8 digits      |
| (310n) | Net weight in kg, n decimals      | 6 digits            |
| (400)  | Customer’s purchase order number  | Up to 30 characters |

## Tips for GS1-128 labels

- **Put fixed-length fields first.** GTIN, dates and weights have fixed lengths, so no separator is needed after them. That keeps the barcode shorter.
- **Your GTIN comes from GS1.** The (01) number is the same GTIN as the product’s retail barcode, padded to 14 digits. Use the number your company licensed.
- **Mind the width.** Every field adds width. A typical pallet label barcode is 100 to 150 mm wide; keep the narrowest bar at least 0.25 mm for warehouse scanners.
- **Human-readable text matters.** The text under the bars shows each field in brackets, which is what logistics labels expect.

For the carton barcode with only a GTIN, use [ITF-14](/barcode-generator/itf-14). For plain labels without GS1 fields, use [Code 128](/barcode-generator/code-128).
