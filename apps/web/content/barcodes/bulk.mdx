## How it works

1. **Pick the type.** UPC, EAN-13, Code 128, GS1-128, Data Matrix, Amazon FNSKU and every other type the [barcode generator](/barcode-generator) makes.
2. **Add your list.** Paste one value per line, or two columns copied from Excel or Google Sheets: the value, then a file name. Or upload a CSV file.
3. **Fix any problems.** Rows with a wrong check digit or a character the type can’t hold are listed by line, so you can correct them in your sheet. We add missing check digits for you.
4. **Download.** A zip with one PNG per barcode, named after the second column or the value. With a paid plan, one PDF with every barcode laid out on Letter or A4 pages, ready to print.

## Amazon FNSKU labels in bulk

Pick **Amazon FNSKU**, paste the FNSKU and product title columns from your Seller Central inventory report, and choose your label size and condition. You get one label per row, sized for a thermal printer or a sheet of labels. Download them as PNG files, or with a paid plan as one PDF or as ZPL for Zebra printers. The [guide to printing FNSKU labels](/guides/how-to-print-amazon-fnsku-labels) covers printer settings.

## Tips for a clean batch

- **Keep numbers as text in your sheet.** Spreadsheets drop leading zeros from UPCs (036000291452 becomes 36000291452). Format the column as text before you paste the numbers in.
- **One type per batch.** Mixing UPCs and Code 128 SKUs in one list will flag the rows that don’t fit; run them as two batches.
- **Name your files.** A second column with the SKU or product name gives each PNG a name you can find later.
- **Check one print.** Before printing hundreds, print one barcode at its final size and scan it with the scanner you’ll use.
