ITF-14 is the barcode on shipping cartons and cases: it encodes a 14-digit GTIN in bars wide enough to print well on corrugated board, with bearer bars around it. Enter your GTIN above; we add or check the check digit and test the scan before you download.

## How to make an ITF-14 barcode

1. Enter the 14-digit GTIN for the carton, or 13 digits and we add the check digit.
2. Keep the default size for corrugated boxes (about 152 mm wide), or scale it down for printed cases on smooth board.
3. Download a PNG, or vector files for your packaging printer (paid plan).

## Building a carton GTIN from your product number

If each carton holds several units of one product, its GTIN-14 is usually:

| Part                                   | Digits | Example                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------- |
| Packaging indicator (which pack size)  | 1      | 1                                 |
| Product number without its check digit | 12     | 540014128876 (from 5400141288766) |
| New check digit                        | 1      | 3 (we calculate it)               |

So a carton of product 5400141288766 might be 15400141288763. Use a different indicator digit for each pack size, and check GS1’s rules for your situation.

<Callout>
  Print ITF-14 on two adjacent sides of the carton if you can, so it can be scanned whichever way
  the box faces on the conveyor.
</Callout>
