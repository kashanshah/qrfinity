A UPC-A barcode encodes the 12-digit Universal Product Code that shop tills scan in the US and Canada. Paste your UPC above: we check the check digit, size the barcode to the retail standard and read it back before you download. PNG and JPG are free.

## How to make a UPC barcode

1. Enter your 12-digit UPC, or the first 11 digits and we add the check digit.
2. Keep the size at 100% (about 37 mm wide) unless your packaging needs it smaller or larger; retail allows 80% to 200%.
3. Download a PNG at print resolution, or an SVG, PDF or EPS for your designer or printer (paid plan).

## Printing tips for UPC barcodes

- **Dark bars on a light background.** Black on white is safest. Red bars don’t scan: many scanners use red light.
- **Keep the margins.** The blank space each side is part of the barcode.
- **Don’t shorten the bars** unless your retailer allows it; shorter bars are harder to scan at an angle.
- **Test a printed proof** with a scanner or a phone barcode app before a big print run.
