UPC-E is a compact barcode for small packages sold in the US and Canada: it writes certain 12-digit UPCs in 6 digits, about 22 mm wide. Enter your UPC-E number above; we check it against the full UPC-A it stands for and test the scan before you download.

## How to make a UPC-E barcode

1. Enter the 6 UPC-E digits, or 7 with the number system, or 8 with the check digit.
2. Check the preview: the number system prints on the left and the check digit on the right.
3. Download a PNG at print resolution, or vector files for your printer (paid plan).

## How UPC-E relates to UPC-A

A scanner reading a UPC-E sends the full 12-digit UPC-A to the till, so prices and stock records only need the UPC-A. For example, UPC-E 04252614 stands for UPC-A 042100005264. That’s why the check digit is calculated from the full number.

For packages with room, a full [UPC-A](/barcode-generator/upc) is simpler. Outside North America, the small-pack barcode is [EAN-8](/barcode-generator/ean-8).
