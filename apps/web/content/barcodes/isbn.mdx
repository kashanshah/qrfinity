An ISBN barcode is the EAN-13 barcode printed on a book’s back cover, built from its ISBN, with the ISBN printed above it. Paste your ISBN above, with or without hyphens, or an old 10-character ISBN: we check it, convert it if needed, and test the scan before you download.

## How to make an ISBN barcode

1. Enter the ISBN for this edition. ISBN-13s start with 978 or 979.
2. Check the preview: the ISBN prints above the bars and the 13 digits below.
3. Download a PNG at print resolution, or a PDF or EPS for your cover designer or printer (paid plan).

## Where it goes on the cover

Put the barcode on the back cover, usually in the lower right corner, on a white or light box. Keep it at least 2 cm from the spine and the edges, and at full size if you can: about 37 mm wide.

<Callout>
Don’t reuse an ISBN across formats or editions. Stores and distributors use it to tell your paperback from your hardback, and a shared number causes ordering mistakes.
</Callout>

An ISBN barcode is an [EAN-13](/barcode-generator/ean-13), so the same printing advice applies: dark bars, a light background and clear margins.
