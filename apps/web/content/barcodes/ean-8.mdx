EAN-8 is the short retail barcode for packages too small for an EAN-13: it encodes an 8-digit product number in about 27 mm. Enter your number above; we add or check the check digit, size it to the standard and test the scan. PNG and JPG are free.

## How to make an EAN-8 barcode

1. Enter your 8-digit EAN-8, or 7 digits and we add the check digit.
2. Keep the standard size if you can, scaling only as far as 80% for very small packs.
3. Download a PNG at print resolution, or vector files for your printer (paid plan).

## Tips for small packaging

- **Flat spots only.** On a tube or a small bottle, run the bars around the curve (vertically) so they don’t bend out of the scanner’s view.
- **High contrast.** Small barcodes have less room for error: black bars on white are safest.
- **Keep the margins.** On tiny packs it’s tempting to crop them; scanners need them.

If your package can fit one, an [EAN-13](/barcode-generator/ean-13) is simpler to get. In the US and Canada, the compact alternative is [UPC-E](/barcode-generator/upc-e).
