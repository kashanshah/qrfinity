Data Matrix is the tiny square code on circuit boards, medical devices, medicine packs and machine parts. It packs text or numbers into a very small space and still reads when part of it is scratched or smudged. Type your text, choose square or rectangular, and download a scan-tested code. PNG and JPG are free.

## Where Data Matrix is used

- **Electronics and parts:** serial numbers on boards, components and tools, often laser-etched.
- **Healthcare:** medicine packs and medical devices, usually as a GS1 Data Matrix carrying the GTIN, batch, expiry and serial number.
- **Small labels:** vials, test tubes, jewellery tags and anywhere a barcode won’t fit.
- **Mail and documents:** postage marks and document tracking.

## Tips for readable Data Matrix codes

- **Keep the data short.** Fewer characters mean fewer, bigger squares at the same size.
- **Leave a margin.** Keep blank space at least one module wide around the code; we include it in the download.
- **High contrast.** Dark on light reads best. Laser etching and dot peen marking need a scanner that handles low contrast.
- **Test at the final size.** Print one, then scan it with the scanner your team uses before marking parts in bulk.
