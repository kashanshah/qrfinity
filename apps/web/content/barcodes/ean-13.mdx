An EAN-13 barcode encodes the 13-digit product number used on retail packaging in most of the world. Enter your number above: we add or check the check digit, size it to the retail standard (about 37 mm wide at 100%) and scan it before you download. PNG and JPG are free.

## How to make an EAN-13 barcode

1. Enter your 13-digit EAN, or 12 digits and we add the check digit.
2. Keep the standard size, or scale it between 80% and 200% to fit your packaging.
3. Turn off the number under the bars only if your design prints it elsewhere; most retailers want it.
4. Download a PNG at print resolution, or vector files for packaging (paid plan).

## Getting EAN-13 barcodes right on packaging

- **Colour:** dark bars (black, dark blue, dark green) on white or a light colour. Avoid red bars and light-on-dark printing.
- **Position:** on a flat area, away from folds, seams and the edge of a curved can or bottle.
- **Margins:** leave the blank space on both sides clear of text and graphics.
- **Proof:** scan a printed proof before the full run; screens hide problems that paper shows.

Books use a special EAN-13 built from the ISBN: use the [ISBN generator](/barcode-generator/isbn). Small packs can use the shorter [EAN-8](/barcode-generator/ean-8).
