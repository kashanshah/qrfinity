To make a QR code, choose what it should open (a link, Wi-Fi, a contact card and so on), fill in the details, adjust the colours and logo if you like, check it scans, and download it. On QRfinity that takes under a minute, needs no sign-up for a static code, and the code never expires.

## Step 1: Choose what the code opens

Open the [generator](/) and pick a type:

- **Link:** a website, menu, form or video. The most common choice.
- **Wi-Fi:** joins a network without typing the password.
- **Contact:** saves your name, phone and email to the phone’s contacts.
- **Email, SMS, Phone, WhatsApp:** starts a message or call.
- **Location, Event:** opens a map or adds an event to the calendar.
- **App store, Social, Payment:** opens an app, a profile or a payment page.

Then fill in the details. The code updates as you type.

## Step 2: Static or dynamic?

- **Static** codes hold the content directly. They work forever with nothing in between, but can’t be changed after printing.
- **Dynamic** codes hold a short link you control. You can change where they go and see how many people scan, with a free account.

If you’re printing something you can’t easily reprint, choose dynamic. More in [static vs dynamic QR codes](/guides/static-vs-dynamic-qr-codes).

## Step 3: Make it yours

Under **Make it yours**, choose a pattern, colours, a logo and a frame with your own words, such as “Scan for the menu”. Keep the code dark on a light background; the [design guide](/guides/qr-code-design-best-practices) explains why.

## Step 4: Check it scans

Every design is checked as you make it: the badge under the preview says **Scans** or **Won’t scan**, with the reason and the smallest size to print. Then scan it with your own phone camera.

## Step 5: Download

Set the print width, then download:

- **PNG** for print at 300 dpi at that width, and for screens.
- **JPG** for places that don’t take PNG.
- **SVG, PDF or EPS** with a paid plan, for designers and print shops.

For a dynamic code, save it to your account first; it then appears in your dashboard with its scan counts.

## Before you print

- Print one copy at the real size and scan it with two phones.
- Check what it opens one last time: a typo in a static code can’t be fixed.
- Keep a clear margin around the code, and see [what size to print it](/guides/qr-code-size-for-print).
