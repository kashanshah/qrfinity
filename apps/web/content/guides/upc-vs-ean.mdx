UPC-A and EAN-13 are the same barcode system with a different number of digits. A UPC-A holds 12 digits and is used mainly in the US and Canada; an EAN-13 holds 13 and is used everywhere else. Every modern retail scanner reads both, so print the one that matches the number GS1 gave you.

## The differences at a glance

|                         | UPC-A                                     | EAN-13                                        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Digits                  | 12                                        | 13                                            |
| Where it’s issued       | GS1 US and GS1 Canada                     | Every other GS1 office                        |
| First digits            | Company prefix                            | Country or GS1 prefix, then company           |
| Read in shops worldwide | Yes                                       | Yes                                           |
| Standard width at 100%  | About 37 mm with margins                  | About 37 mm with margins                      |
| Make one                | [UPC-A generator](/barcode-generator/upc) | [EAN-13 generator](/barcode-generator/ean-13) |

## Why there are two

UPC came first, in the US in the 1970s. Europe then extended it to 13 digits so other countries could have their own prefixes: that became EAN. A UPC is really an EAN-13 that starts with 0, which is why scanners read UPCs as 13 digits and why the two are fully compatible.

## The small versions

- **[UPC-E](/barcode-generator/upc-e)** squeezes certain UPC-A numbers (those with runs of zeros) into 6 digits for small packages. Only some numbers can be compressed.
- **[EAN-8](/barcode-generator/ean-8)** is a separate 8-digit number that GS1 issues on request for packs too small for an EAN-13.

## Which should I print?

Print the one that matches your number. If GS1 US or GS1 Canada gave you a 12-digit number, print a UPC-A. If your GS1 office gave you 13 digits, print an EAN-13. If a retailer asks for a 12-digit UPC and you have a 13-digit EAN starting with 0, drop the 0.

For books, the barcode is always an EAN-13 built from the ISBN; see the [ISBN barcode generator](/barcode-generator/isbn).
